Internship Guidelines

Please review our rules and frequently asked questions before submitting your application.

1. Project Requirements

You are required to complete any two levels of your chosen domain (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced). Alternatively, you can complete one level and write a blog post relevant to your field.

  • All work must be your own. Plagiarism will result in immediate termination.
  • Ensure all required files (source code, report) are submitted via the official portal before the deadline.
  • Your report must include screenshots of your working projects.

2. AI Usage Policy

You may use AI tools (like ChatGPT) for guidance, learning, and debugging. However, you may not use AI to write your entire project or your final report. Your submission should reflect your own understanding.

3. Certification

Your Internship Completion Certificate will only be issued after your project has been successfully submitted, evaluated, and found to comply with all guidelines.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I do the internship remotely? +
Yes, the entire internship is remote and can be completed from anywhere.
How long do I have to complete the tasks? +
You have 4 weeks from the date of your acceptance to complete and submit your projects.
Can I share my project on GitHub? +
You may use a private GitHub repository for version control. Do not make it public until the internship has officially ended.
